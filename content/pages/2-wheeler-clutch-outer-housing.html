Engineering Pillars

Clutch Housing Engineering Highlights

Pillar 1

Die-Cast Aluminium Rigidity

High-density alloy die-casting resists torsional flexing and prevents bell housing expansion under high RPM acceleration.

Advanced fin geometry and premium thermal conductivity channel friction heat away from internal plates, preventing clutch fade.

Precision CNC turned and dynamically balanced to eliminate high-speed drivetrain vibration and minimise engine bearing stress.
