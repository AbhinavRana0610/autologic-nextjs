About Autologic Industries: India’s Trusted Automotive Component Manufacturer

Autologic Industries is a leading Indian manufacturer of precision two wheeler, three-wheeler, and general automotive components. Built on the belief that "the smallest components hold the greatest responsibility," we specialise in areas like clutch systems and precision die casting. Our growth is driven by constant reinvestment in advanced CNC machinery and IATF-certified quality control, making us a Tier-1 supplier and reliable exporter to numerous global manufacturers.
