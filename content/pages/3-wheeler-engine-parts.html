Maintenance Guide

Tips for a Longer Engine Life

Good parts last longest when the engine is looked after. These simple habits help drivers and fleet owners protect their investment and avoid unplanned stops.

  • Change engine oil and filter at the recommended interval
  • Clean the air filter often in dusty conditions
  • Watch for smoke, knocking or loss of power
  • Replace worn parts as a set, such as rings with pistons
