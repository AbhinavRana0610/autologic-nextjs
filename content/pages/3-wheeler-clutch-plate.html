Why Autologic

Why Choose Our Three-Wheeler Clutch Plates

The clutch plate is the part that wears first in any auto rickshaw. Every start, every gear change and every climb with a full load rubs its friction lining against the flywheel and pressure plate. As a dedicated three-wheeler clutch plate manufacturer, we focus on friction material, rivet strength and damper design so each plate grips firmly and lasts longer between replacements.

Engineering

Friction That Lasts

Our linings use a heat-stable friction compound that keeps a steady grip in crowded traffic, when the clutch is slipped again and again. Linings are bonded and riveted to a hardened steel core plate, so they do not crack or separate under heavy torque.

Torsional damper springs in the hub absorb shock when the driver releases the pedal quickly, protecting the gearbox and giving passengers a smoother ride.

  • Heat-stable, low-fade friction lining
  • Hardened steel core plate and strong rivets
  • Damper springs for smooth engagement
  • Accurate spline fit on the input shaft

Maintenance Guide

When to Replace a Three-Wheeler Clutch Plate

A worn clutch plate wastes fuel and slows the vehicle on every trip. Watching for early signs helps drivers and fleet owners plan the job before a breakdown on the road.

Warning Signs

  • Engine revs rise but speed does not
  • Clutch bites very high on the pedal
  • Hard gear changes or grinding noise
  • Shudder when starting on a slope

Fitting Tips

Fit the plate with the correct side facing the flywheel, centre it with an alignment tool and check the pressure plate and release bearing at the same time. A matched set gives the longest service life.
