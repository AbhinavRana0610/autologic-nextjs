Why Autologic

Why Choose Our Three-Wheeler Pressure Plates

A pressure plate decides how firmly the clutch holds under load. In an auto rickshaw that carries passengers all day or hauls cargo up steep flyovers, a weak or warped plate causes slipping, burnt linings and costly breakdowns. As an experienced three-wheeler pressure plate manufacturer, we build every plate to keep clamping force steady from the first kilometre to the last.

Engineering

Built for Heavy Daily Duty

Our plates are cast from high-grade alloy, machined flat on CNC lathes and paired with calibrated springs, so pressure stays even across the whole friction surface. This prevents hot spots, glazing and judder during stop-and-go city driving.

Each design is matched to the clutch cover, diaphragm or coil springs and release bearing of the target model, giving smooth engagement and a light, consistent pedal feel for drivers who shift hundreds of times a day.

Maintenance Guide

When to Replace a Three-Wheeler Pressure Plate

Mechanics and fleet owners should inspect the pressure plate whenever the clutch is opened. Replacing the plate together with the clutch plate saves a second labour job and protects the new friction lining.

Warning Signs

  • Clutch slipping when climbing with a full load
  • Burning smell after short city trips
  • Judder or vibration while pulling away
  • Blue heat marks or cracks on the plate face

Fitting Tips

Check the flywheel face, tighten the cover bolts evenly in a cross pattern and adjust the cable free play after fitting. These simple steps let a new plate deliver its full service life.
