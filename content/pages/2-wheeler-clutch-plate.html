Engineering Pillars

Friction Plate Engineering Highlights

Pillar 1

High-Coefficient Friction

Advanced composite matrix delivers instantaneous lever bite, zero clutch slip, and crisp power transmission to the gearbox, even when the bike is fully loaded.

Pillar 2

Thermal Stability

Formulated to resist plate glazing, warp, and oil contamination during continuous stop-go commuting in extreme ambient heat, keeping the lever feel consistent for longer.
