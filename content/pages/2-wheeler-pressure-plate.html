Why Autologic

Why Choose Our Clutch Pressure Plates

The pressure plate squeezes the clutch pack together every time the rider lets the lever out. If it is not flat or its spring pads are uneven, the clutch slips, drags or judders. As a two wheeler clutch pressure plate manufacturer, we control flatness, hardness and pad height on every plate, so the clutch feels smooth from the first ride.

Built and Checked In-House

Plates are stamped, heat treated and surface ground in our own plant, then inspected for flatness, thickness and run-out. Sample plates are fitted into test clutch packs to confirm lever feel and engagement.

  • Flatness and run-out inspection on every lot
  • Hardness checks after heat treatment
  • Rust-preventive finish for long storage

When to Replace It

Replace the pressure plate when it shows blue heat marks, scoring or warping, or when a new friction plate set still slips. Fitting a fresh plate with new friction discs gives the longest clutch life.

  • Clutch slips under hard acceleration
  • Lever feel becomes uneven or heavy
  • Visible scoring or heat discolouration
